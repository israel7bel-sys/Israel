About the studio

Faith learned to braid at eleven, on her sisters' heads.

Years and hundreds of clients later, that same care shows up in every part and every strand — sectioning done properly, tension checked as she goes, and styles that are made to last without pulling on your hairline. Style by Faith is a one-chair studio built on word of mouth: come in, sit down, and leave with something you'll want to show off.
